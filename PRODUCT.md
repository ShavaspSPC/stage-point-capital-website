# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Accredited and qualified investors evaluating a secured promissory note offering from Stage Point Master, LLC. Typically reviewing on a desktop between other work, arriving via referral, an advisor, or the existing Stage Point Capital investor network rather than cold organic search. They already have some familiarity with private credit or real estate lending and are comparing this offering against bonds, CDs, or other private debt funds. They are financially literate but not necessarily technical; they want the mechanics of the deal (terms, protection, track record) laid out plainly, not dumbed down or oversold. The job to be done is due-diligence-at-a-glance: confirm the offering is real, understand the terms, verify the protections, meet the team, and decide whether to request the offering memorandum or a call.

## Product Purpose

A single-page investor landing site that moves a qualified visitor from first scroll to one of two actions: request the offering memorandum, or speak with the team. It exists to give Stage Point Master's note offering a credible, institutional-grade digital presence that matches the seriousness of a 12-year, $205M-plus lending track record, rather than relying solely on a PDF deck passed hand to hand. Success is a qualified investor who leaves the page understanding the yield structure, the collateral protection, and the people behind the fund, and who takes the CTA.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, transparent, understated. The voice is professional, positive, and insightful: confident without boasting, speaking to investors as equals rather than prospects to be sold. Every stated challenge (banking retreat, aging housing stock) pairs with how Stage Point addresses it. Numbers do the persuading, not adjectives. The emotional target is quiet confidence and trust, the feeling of reading a well-prepared offering memorandum, not a startup pitch deck.

## Anti-references

- Generic crypto/fintech hype sites: neon gradients, countdown timers, glassmorphism panels, oversized emoji-style icons, "APY" energy.
- Generic SaaS startup landing pages: purple gradient blobs, bouncy illustrations, "Get Started Free" urgency, stacked logo walls, hollow buzzwords (disrupt, crush, dominate, hustle, killer, ninja, guru).
- No em dashes as sentence punctuation, no exclamation marks, no all-caps shouting, no emojis anywhere on the page.
- Nothing that reads as templated: no default hero-with-stock-photo-and-two-buttons layout without a specific point of view.

## Design Principles

- **Numbers carry the argument.** Track record and protection figures (50th consecutive quarter, zero principal loss since 2014, ~30% equity buffer) are the persuasion; copy frames them, it does not replace them.
- **Show the mechanics, don't just assert safety.** The payment waterfall, capital structure, and underwriting controls are demonstrated as concrete structures (diagrams, tiers), not summarized as vague reassurance.
- **Restraint signals institutional trust.** A conservative navy-and-steel-teal palette, generous whitespace, and calm motion communicate more credibility to this audience than visual excitement would.
- **One primary action, repeated consistently.** Every section reinforces the same two CTAs (request the offering memorandum, speak with the team) rather than competing for attention with secondary asks.
- **Compliant by construction.** Every performance claim is scoped and sourced from the offering materials; the disclaimer is treated as a first-class section, not legal boilerplate bolted on at the end.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: sufficient color contrast for navy-on-white and teal accent text, full keyboard navigation for nav and CTAs, visible focus states, semantic heading structure, and alt text on all imagery (team headshots, hero photography). All scroll-triggered and count-up animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and degrade to an immediate, static state with no loss of information.
